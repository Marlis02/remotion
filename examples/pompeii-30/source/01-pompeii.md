# chapter: main

## scene: eruption

[img: last-day] In the year seventy-nine, Vesuvius tore itself open above the Bay of Naples. [beat: noon] By afternoon the sky had gone black, and stone was falling on the roofs of Pompeii.

## scene: buried

[img: excavation] The city was not burned. [pause: 300ms] It was buried under four metres of ash, and it stayed there for seventeen centuries. What the ash covered, it kept. [pause: 250ms] About two thousand people never got out.

## scene: cast

[img: cast] The bodies decayed and left hollows in the ash. [beat: plaster] Pour plaster into a hollow, and the person comes back, a shape of the last moment. [emph] Eighteen centuries later.
