# chapter: main

## scene: street

[img: street] Mulberry Street, New York, nineteen hundred. Under every doorway on this block there is a cellar, and by winter most of them will be workshops. [beat: cellars] A tailor from Naples rents the corner one for four dollars a month. No window, one lamp, a table cut from a door.

## scene: work

[img: mechanic] Twenty years on, the cellars have grown machines. A press, a lathe, a steam pump shaking the grocer's floor upstairs. [pause: 300ms] He is not hiding down here. [beat: shop] He is building the only shop he can afford, and the [emph] shop is winning.

## scene: road

[img: mother] Nineteen thirty-six. The children of those cellars are on the road, carrying what a basement taught them. A trade in your hands is the one thing [beat: evict] [emph] nobody can evict.
